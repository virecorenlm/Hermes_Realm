# Vire — Content Strategy & Scheduling

## Example Post Frequency Targets

These are illustrative planning values. Choose a schedule for the configured store and obtain approval before publishing.

- **TikTok**: 1x daily, 6–9 PM local time
- **Instagram**: 4–5x weekly (Reels priority), 11 AM or 7 PM
- **Facebook**: 3–4x weekly, mix of product + lifestyle + fishing tips

## Content Pillars for the configured store

1. **Product Spotlight** — New arrivals, featured lures/rods/tackle
2. **Fishing Tips** — Local spots, seasonal species, bait selection
3. **Customer Catches** — UGC reposts, community highlights
4. **Behind the Store** — Day-in-life, inventory days, humor
5. **Promotions** — Sales, bundles, seasonal deals

## Content Naming Convention
```
YYYY-MM-DD_[platform]_[pillar]_[version].[ext]
# Example:
2025-06-01_tiktok_fishing-tips_v1.mp4
2025-06-01_ig_product-spotlight_v1.jpg
```
